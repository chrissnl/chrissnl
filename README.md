### 🚀 My Microsoft to GitHub journey
- Following undergrad in 2015, I began my Microsoft career as a Technical Support Engineer, directly supporting our Enterprise customers with their Exchange Server & Online deployments.
  > - Highlight: Solving a critsit and celebrating with the customer!
- From 2016-2018, I made a shift to the MS Worldwide Services organzation reporting to the Chief of Staff, driving special initiatives around the employee experience.
  > - Highlight: Scaling our annual Circle of Excellence recognition program - rewarding people for their achievements felt like a reward in itself.
- In 2020, at the height of the Covid-19 pandemic and the world moving online, I dove into this new remote-first world by driving MS Digital's customer engagement program. In this role, I supported our Enterprise customers through Digital Transformation solutions by connecting them with the SMEs scaling it within our own company.
  > - Highlight: Working in tandem with our customers to rapidly - and securely - deploy remote collaboration on a global scale. It still feels like a once-in-a-lifetime career story.
- This remote-first role naturally led me to the 👑 of async team collaboration - GitHub. And the rest is still pen on paper ✍️ [Connect](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chrissnl/) with me to follow along.

### 👩🏻‍💻 How I do my best work 
- I am an advocate of block scheduling and organizing my calendar with focus time for task execution. Please respect the blocks noted as "Do not schedule" - the rest are open for scheduling time to collaborate live!
- I follow an 80:20 method for async:live work - collaborating asynchronously allows both you and I to maximize our day. For the 20%, I enjoy reviewing and brainstorming ideas, or collaborating on solutions. 
- I am an INFJ in the MBTI world - the "Advocate". I am driven by advocating for continuous people/systems/process growth and improvement - even if it's by 1%!
- I thrive in an environment that has a "North Star" mission, and where the journey lends itself to creative solutions, cross-team collaboration, and ships to learn.
